What happened

  • Revenue was $4.07M, +17.2% versus the same days last year (+$598K).
  • Comparable sales (store-products on the shelf every day of both periods, 99% of revenue) grew +15.9%: volume +$489K, mix +$41K, price +$20K.
  • Listing changes (new, returning and dropped store-products) added +$48K.
  • By category, Foods led on comparable sales (+19.1%) and Hobbies was weakest (+6.1%).
  • 2 of the top fallers (FOODS_3_444, FOODS_3_441) had a shelf price all month but sold nothing: likely out of stock or quietly delisted. Worth checking first.
  • Store stock-outs so far: $27K. This is a floor, not a final number: detection needs 28 days of later sales, so runs near the end of the data can't be confirmed yet.
  • Fiscal 2017 to date: $12.18M, +15.9% versus the same days last year.
